Why this matters

Not every caregiver is a nurse.

An RN&RM (Registered Nurse & Registered Midwife) has completed a recognised GNM or B.Sc Nursing and is registered with a State Nursing Council — that registration is the licence to practise. Attendants and ward boys are not nurses and cannot legally perform clinical procedures. So registration plus experience plus a specialisation is not something every caregiver can offer.
